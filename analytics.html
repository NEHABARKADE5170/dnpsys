Manufacturing Execution System (MES)

DNP Systems provides intelligent analytics solutions that transform industrial data into meaningful insights. By collecting data from PLCs, sensors, machines, and control systems, our solutions enable performance monitoring, production analysis, traceability, reporting, and data-driven decision making.

Data Acquisition

Collect and manage real-time data from PLCs, sensors, and multiple control systems for centralized monitoring.

System Integration

Seamlessly integrate multiple PLCs, databases, and industrial automation systems into a unified MES platform.

OEE & Performance Analytics

Track Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE), production efficiency, downtime, and key performance indicators in real time.

Digital Product Traceability

Create digital product profiles with QR code integration for complete production traceability and lifecycle management.

Reports & Dashboards

Develop customized dashboards, reports, graphs, widgets, and tables to provide actionable operational insights.

Workforce & Equipment Monitoring

Monitor OEE, production efficiency, KPIs, operator performance, equipment utilization, and downtime in real time to optimize manufacturing operations.

Software Configuration

Configure and deploy MES software tailored to specific manufacturing processes and operational requirements.

Enterprise Reporting Portal

Deliver a centralized portal with role-based access for secure visualization and reporting of plant operations and production data.
